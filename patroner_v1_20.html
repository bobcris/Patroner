Grading increments are explicit between adjacent sizes. These profiles regenerate each size from measurements; they are not yet a validated production-grade multi-size block.

Point grading is experimental: it applies measured size differences to defined draft landmarks, not a certified industrial grading rule.

Draft landmarks

Landmarks are extracted from the current generated panel. They are diagnostic anchors for future grading rules, not certified grading points.

Size progression audit

Checks the provisional XS–XXL chart for positive measurements, monotonic waist/hip progression, and expected quarter-panel arithmetic. This is a regression audit, not a fit or grading certification.
